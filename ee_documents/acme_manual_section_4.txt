ACME Industrial Electrical Installation Manual - Section 4: Motor Branch Circuits

According to the ACME Industrial Electrical Installation Manual, Section 4.3.2, motor branch circuits feeding induction motors rated above 5 kW must use Type D curve miniature circuit breakers rather than Type C, due to the high inrush current during direct-on-line (DOL) starting. For motors rated between 0.75 kW and 5 kW, Type C curve breakers are acceptable, as their inrush current is lower and better matched to Type C's tripping characteristic. Additionally, per Section 4.3.5, all motor branch circuit conductors must be sized for a minimum of 125% of the motor's full-load current rating, in accordance with the manual's derating table for continuous-duty loads. This 125% margin is separate from, and in addition to, any voltage-drop-based conductor sizing requirement in Section 5.1.
